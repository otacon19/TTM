Compare movies

The movies are compared in pairs using values between 1 and 9 (you can find out the name of the movie by placing your mouse over the word 'Movie'). You should provide your preferences over the movies considering the following:

If you prefer the movie on the left, choose a value using the red scale.

If you prefer the movie on the right, choose a value using the green scale.

Notice 9 means 'Extremely much better", 1 means "Equal" and the remainder values represent intermediate values of preference.
